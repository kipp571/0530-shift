0530 Shift: 10-Week Master Curriculum

High-yield plan to build clinical discipline, covering Med-Surg, Midwifery, Pediatrics, Forensic Nursing, Research, and Multimedia Health.

Weeks Overview

  • Week 1: Foundations & Maternal-Fetal

    • Mon: Communication (SBAR)
    • Tue: Pharma (Magnesium Sulfate / Preeclampsia)
    • Wed: GK (Blue Zones & Longevity)
    • Thu: Midwifery (Leopold’s Maneuvers)
    • Fri: Gyn (Cervical Cancer & HPV)

  • Week 2: Cardiac & Forensic Link

    • Mon: Med-Surg (ECG Basics)
    • Tue: Forensic (ER: Evidence Preservation)
    • Wed: GK (History of the Stethoscope)
    • Thu: Med-Surg (Myocardial Infarction: MONA Protocol)
    • Fri: Forensic (Legal vs. Clinical Charting)

  • Week 3: Pediatrics & Childhood Emergencies

    • Mon: Growth & Development (Erikson’s Stages)
    • Tue: Dehydrated Child: Fluid Bolus Calculation
    • Wed: Multimedia (Video Patient Education)
    • Thu: Respiratory Distress: Retractions & Grunting
    • Fri: GK (Tele-pediatrics)

  • Week 4: Midwifery & Gynaecology Deep Dive

    • Mon: Stages of Labor (Latent vs. Active)
    • Tue: Endometriosis & Patient Advocacy
    • Wed: Medical Ethics (Tuskegee Study)
    • Thu: Postpartum Hemorrhage (Fundal Massage & Oxytocin)
    • Fri: PCOS: Metabolic & Hormonal Nursing Care

  • Week 5-10: Med-Surg, Research, Forensic, Multimedia & Integration

    • Week 5: Research & EBP
    • Week 6: Med-Surg Mastery (Renal & Fluids)
    • Week 7: Forensic Nursing & Trauma
    • Week 8: Pediatrics & Health Multimedia
    • Week 9: Midwifery & High-Risk Pregnancy
    • Week 10: Integration & Final Shift
